# HandPoint downloads

**Made by Kelvin Thale. © 2026 Kelvin Thale. All rights reserved.**

HandPoint lets you control an Android phone by moving your hand in front of it: a cursor follows your hand, and
pinches become taps, scrolls and Back. Everything runs on the phone; the app has no internet permission.

**Download the latest version:** [HandPoint.apk](https://github.com/crazykelt75-eng/handpoint-releases/releases/latest/download/HandPoint.apk)
(Android 11 or newer).

Install and setup steps are on the [release page](https://github.com/crazykelt75-eng/handpoint-releases/releases/latest).
Updating from 0.2.x: uninstall the old version first (0.3.0 and later are signed with a new key).
