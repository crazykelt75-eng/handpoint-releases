Phone Mic

Talk into your phone and hear it on your Bluetooth speaker.

Connect your Bluetooth speaker, then tap Start.

Echo blocker

When you tap Start, the speaker makes two short hisses so the page can measure the echo.

Tap this after you move the phone or the speaker.
Hearing an echo?
  1. Hold the phone close to your mouth, about a hand's width away.
  2. Put the speaker a few steps away from you, facing away from the phone.
  3. Set Echo blocker to Strong, or lower the volume a little.
  4. Still there? Turn on "Phone's own echo cancelling".
  5. If you hear each word only once, a split second late, that's the Bluetooth delay. Every Bluetooth speaker has it, and no app can remove it. Only a speaker plugged in with a cable avoids it. People listening across the room don't notice it much.
Tips if it doesn't work
  1. Pair and connect the Bluetooth speaker first, and play any music to check sound comes out of it.
  2. Open this link in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), not inside another app's built-in browser.
  3. If it asks for microphone permission, tap Allow. If you blocked it, tap the icon next to the web address, open Permissions, and allow Microphone.
  4. Keep this page open and the screen on while you use it.
  5. Sound is thin, like a phone call? Pick the phone's own microphone in the Microphone list, not the speaker's, and turn off "Phone's own echo cancelling".
